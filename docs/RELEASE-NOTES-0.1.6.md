## Linear, compact Windows workflow

The desktop app now follows the natural sequence: prepare the local study space, sign in to KU Leuven, discover courses, then select and synchronize material. A compact step bar shows progress, and actions remain unavailable until their prerequisites are complete. The activity monitor remains fixed at the bottom of the window.
